## 1.3 Pronunciation: Individual Letters

German spelling is much more consistent and phonetic than English. However, several letters change their sound depending on their position in a word, or are pronounced differently from English.

### 1. Final Devoicing: `b`, `d`, `g`

At the **start** of words and syllables, `b`, `d`, and `g` sound just like they do in English. However, at the **end** of a word or syllable (standing alone or in a consonant cluster), they "harden" (devoice):

- **`b` is pronounced as `[p]`**
- **`d` is pronounced as `[t]`**
- **`g` is pronounced as `[k]`** (or `[h]` after _i_, covered on Day 4)

Because of this rule, many differently spelled words become **homophones** (they sound identical!):

### 3. The Three Rules of `s` (`[z]`, `[s]`, and `[sh]`)

In German, the letter **s** behaves the exact opposite way to English depending on where it sits in a word:

#### A. `s` = `[z]` (like 'z' in _busy_ / _is_)

Occurs at the **start of a word or syllable** (before a vowel) or between vowels:

- **Book Examples:** **[sein](https://www.deepl.com/translator#de/en/sein)** (to be / his), **[Symbol](https://www.deepl.com/translator#de/en/Symbol)** (symbol), **[Rose](https://www.deepl.com/translator#de/en/Rose)** (rose), **[Riese](https://www.deepl.com/translator#de/en/Riese)** (giant).
- **Example Sentence:** _Die **[Rose](https://www.deepl.com/translator#de/en/Rose)** **[ist](https://www.deepl.com/translator#de/en/ist)** **[sehr](https://www.deepl.com/translator#de/en/sehr)** schön._ (The rose is very beautiful.)

#### B. `s` = `[s]` (like 's' in _sits_ / _its_)

Occurs at the **end of a word or syllable**:

- **Book Examples:** **[Gast](https://www.deepl.com/translator#de/en/Gast)** (guest), **[längst](https://www.deepl.com/translator#de/en/l%C3%A4ngst)** (long since), **[Wespe](https://www.deepl.com/translator#de/en/Wespe)** (wasp), **[Muster](https://www.deepl.com/translator#de/en/Muster)** (pattern), **[meins](https://www.deepl.com/translator#de/en/meins)** (mine).
- **Example Sentence:** **\*[Dieses](https://www.deepl.com/translator#de/en/Dieses)** Eis ist **[süß](https://www.deepl.com/translator#de/en/s%C3%BC%C3%9F)**, sagt der **[Gast](https://www.deepl.com/translator#de/en/Gast)**.\* (This ice cream is sweet, says the guest.)

#### C. `sp` and `st` = `[shp]` and `[sht]`

When **sp** or **st** appear at the **start of a word or syllable**, the **s** is pronounced like the English **'sh'** (`[sh]`):

- **Book Examples (`sp`):** **[Speck](https://www.deepl.com/translator#de/en/Speck)** (bacon), **[spät](https://www.deepl.com/translator#de/en/sp%C3%A4t)** (late), **[spülen](https://www.deepl.com/translator#de/en/sp%C3%BClen)** (to rinse/wash dishes).
- **Book Examples (`st`):** **[Stamm](https://www.deepl.com/translator#de/en/Stamm)** (tree trunk/tribe), **[sterben](https://www.deepl.com/translator#de/en/sterben)** (to die), **[steigen](https://www.deepl.com/translator#de/en/steigen)** (to climb/rise).
- **Example Sentence:** _Es ist **[spät](https://www.deepl.com/translator#de/en/sp%C3%A4t)**, wir **[stehen](https://www.deepl.com/translator#de/en/stehen)** auf der **[Straße](https://www.deepl.com/translator#de/en/Stra%C3%9Fe)**._ (It is late, we are standing on the street.)

## 🗣️ Mini Speaking Drill (Read Aloud!)

Test your mastery of today's consonant rules by reading these 5 sentences out loud:

1. **Zwei** `[tsvy]` **Jungen** `[yoong-en]` **sitzen** `[zit-sen]` im **Wagen** `[vah-gen]`.  
   _(Two boys are sitting in the car.)_
2. Der **Vater** `[fah-tuh]` **wohnt** `[vohnt]` in **Deutschland** `[doych-lahnt]`.  
   _(The father lives in Germany.)_
3. **Spät** `[shpeht]` am **Abend** `[ah-bent]` hat das **Pferd** `[pfeht]` **Hunger** `[hoong-uh]`.  
   _(Late in the evening, the horse is hungry.)_
4. **Dieses** `[dee-zes]` **Mehl** `[mehl]` hat gute **Qualität** `[kvah-li-teht]`.  
   _(This flour has good quality.)_
5. **Tschüs** `[chues]`, bis **später** `[shpeh-tuh]` an der **Station** `[shtah-tsiohn]`!  
   _(Bye, see you later at the station!)_
