# 🇩🇪 German in 3 Months – Complete 13-Week Master Plan

Welcome to your comprehensive roadmap for mastering German! This guide outlines your complete day-by-day curriculum based on the *Hugo German in 3 Months* course.

---

## ⏱️ Daily Study Routine (Approx. 1 Hour/Day)

To get the absolute most out of this schedule, follow this structured daily workflow:

1. **Warm-up (10 minutes):** Always spend the first 10 minutes revising what you learned the day before.
2. **Grammar & New Material (20 minutes):** Read the new section carefully, ensuring you fully understand the grammatical construction.
3. **Audio & Vocabulary (15 minutes):** Listen to the accompanying audio on the official **DK Hugo in 3 Months app** and repeat sample sentences and vocabulary out loud.
4. **Exercises & Conversations (15 minutes):** Complete the exercises at the end of the section and check your answers against the key at the back of the book. At the end of each week, listen to the conversation first, then read it aloud.

## Month 1: Foundations, Cases, and Everyday Basics (Weeks 1–4)

### Week 1: Pronunciation & Spelling (Pages 9–24)
* **Goal:** Master the sounds of the German language without worrying about word meanings yet.
* **Day 1:** Sections 1.1 – 1.2 (Speaking German, The alphabet, umlauts, *ß*, and capitalization rules).
* **Day 2:** Section 1.3 (Pronunciation of individual letters and consonant blocks).
* **Day 3:** Section 1.4 (Long and short vowels, diphthongs, unstressed syllables).
* **Day 4:** Section 1.5 (The consonants *ch*, *l*, and *r*).
* **Day 5:** Sections 1.6 – 1.8 (The hiatus/pause, word stress, and German punctuation).
* **Day 6 & 7:** Full review of Week 1 audio tracks, repeating all example words out loud.

### Week 2: Articles, Gender, and Plurals (Pages 25–31)
* **Goal:** Learn everyday greetings, noun genders (masculine, feminine, neuter), and how to form plurals.
* **Day 1:** Sections 2.1 – 2.2 (Inflection, word order, greetings) & Exercise 1 dialogue.
* **Day 2:** Section 2.3 (The definite article *der, die, das*) & Exercise 2 (House and family vocabulary).
* **Day 3:** Sections 2.4 – 2.5 (The plural form of 'the' and forming noun plurals).
* **Day 4:** Exercise 3 (Practising plural forms).
* **Day 5–7:** Memorize the gender and plural forms of all 30 nouns from Week 2 and review.

### Week 3: Cases, Pronouns, and Present Tense Verbs (Pages 32–38)
* **Goal:** Understand subject vs. direct object (nominative vs. accusative) and conjugate essential verbs.
* **Day 1:** Sections 3.1 – 3.2 (Grammatical case, declension of *der/die/das*, and personal pronouns).
* **Day 2:** Section 3.3 (Present tense of *sein* 'to be', *haben* 'to have', and regular verbs like *machen* 'to make').
* **Day 3:** Exercise 1 (Learn 8 new verbs and translate sentences).
* **Day 4:** Week 3 Vocabulary list (Asking for directions).
* **Day 5:** Conversation (*An encounter in the street*).
* **Day 6 & 7:** Weekly revision and audio repetition.

### Week 4: Indefinite Articles, Numbers, Prices, and the Dative Case (Pages 39–52)
* **Goal:** Count up to a million, talk about prices, and use indirect objects (dative case).
* **Day 1:** Section 4.1 (The indefinite article *ein* and its negative *kein*) & Exercise 1.
* **Day 2:** Section 4.2 (Numbers 0 to 1,000,000 and years).
* **Day 3:** Section 4.3 (Prices and currency) & Exercise 2.
* **Day 4:** Sections 4.4 – 4.5 (The dative case/indirect object and verbs taking the dative) & Exercise 3.
* **Day 5:** Vocabulary & Conversation (*A problem of etiquette at the florist's*).
* **Day 6 & 7:** Review Month 1 concepts (Nominative, Accusative, Dative tables).

## Month 2: Sentence Structure, Prepositions, Adjectives, and Past Tense (Weeks 5–8)

### Week 5: Questions, Negatives, Commands, and Adjectives/Adverbs (Pages 53–66)
* **Goal:** Ask questions, give commands, form negative sentences with *nicht*, and compare things.
* **Day 1:** Section 5.1 (Asking yes/no questions, question words, *Was für ein?*) & Exercise 1.
* **Day 2:** Sections 5.2 – 5.3 (Negative sentences with *nicht* and the imperative/command form).
* **Day 3:** Vocabulary 1 & Exercise 2 (Translation: *Buying a town guide*).
* **Day 4:** Section 5.4 (Adjectives, adverbs, comparatives, and superlatives) & Exercise 3.
* **Day 5:** Vocabulary 2 & Conversation (*Showing vacant flats to a prospective tenant*).
* **Day 6 & 7:** Weekly revision and audio practice.

### Week 6: Prepositions and 'Es gibt' (Pages 67–75)
* **Goal:** Master how prepositions trigger the accusative, dative, or both cases.
* **Day 1:** Section 6.1 parts 1 & 2 (Prepositions taking the accusative vs. dative, and contracted forms).
* **Day 2:** Section 6.1 parts 3, 4 & 5 (Two-way prepositions, *bis*, and *gegenüber*).
* **Day 3:** Vocabulary 1, Exercise 1, and Exercise 2.
* **Day 4:** Section 6.2 (The expression *es gibt* - 'there is/are').
* **Day 5:** Vocabulary 2 & Exercise 3 (*In the lounge of a hotel*).
* **Day 6 & 7:** Memorize the preposition groups and their required cases.

### Week 7: Possessives, Adjective Endings, Ordinals, and Word Order (Pages 76–89)
* **Goal:** Decline adjectives before nouns, use *du/ihr*, and master the "verb-second" rule.
* **Day 1:** Sections 7.1 – 7.2 (*dieser/jeder/welcher* and possessive adjectives *mein/dein/unser*) & Exercise 1.
* **Day 2:** Section 7.3 (Adjective endings before nouns: Sets 1, 2, and 3).
* **Day 3:** Sections 7.4 – 7.5 (Ordinal numbers, fractions, and informal address *du/ihr*).
* **Day 4:** Section 7.6 (Word order: keeping the verb second) & Exercise 2.
* **Day 5:** Vocabulary, Conversation (*Putting off the shopping*), and Exercises 3 & 4.
* **Day 6 & 7:** Intensive review of adjective declension tables.

### Week 8: Modal Verbs, Quantities, Position Verbs, and 'da(r)-' (Pages 90–106)
* **Goal:** Use modal verbs (*können, müssen, wollen*), express quantities, and distinguish position verbs (*stehen/stellen, liegen/legen*).
* **Day 1:** Sections 8.1 – 8.3 (Word order with two verbs, conjugating and using the 8 auxiliary/modal verbs).
* **Day 2:** Vocabulary 1, Conversation (*Discussing the menu*), and Exercise 1.
* **Day 3:** Sections 8.4 – 8.7 (Measurements/quantities, *es ist/es sind*, and verbs for 'to be' vs. 'to put' indicating position).
* **Day 4:** Section 8.8 (*da(r)-* + preposition) & Exercise 2.
* **Day 5:** Vocabulary 2 & Exercises 3 & 4 (*Leaving food in the refrigerator*).
* **Day 6 & 7:** Review Month 2 concepts and practice oral exercises.

## Month 3: Advanced Tenses, Complex Sentences, Subjunctive, and Fluency (Weeks 9–13)

### Week 9: Irregular Present Verbs, Present Perfect Tense, and Time (Pages 107–132)
* **Goal:** Talk about the past using the present perfect tense and master dates, times, and prefixes.
* **Day 1:** Sections 9.1 – 9.3 (*der/die/das* as pronouns, uses of the present tense, irregular present tense verbs) & Exercise 1.
* **Day 2:** Sections 9.4 – 9.6 (Forming the present perfect, choosing *sein* vs. *haben*, uses of the present perfect).
* **Day 3:** Section 9.7 (Irregular past participles, inseparable vs. separable prefixes, *-ieren* verbs) & Exercise 2.
* **Day 4:** Sections 9.8 – 9.9 (Time expressions, years, months, dates, telling time, *nicht wahr?*) & Exercise 3.
* **Day 5:** Vocabulary & Conversation (*A doctor receives a new patient*).
* **Day 6 & 7:** Memorize irregular past participles from Section 9.7.

### Week 10: Genitive Case, Infinitive Clauses, Passive Voice, and Simple Past (Pages 133–154)
* **Goal:** Express possession (genitive), use *um/ohne/statt ... zu*, form the passive voice, and use the simple past of modals.
* **Day 1:** Sections 10.1 – 10.3 (The genitive case, genitive prepositions, and masculine n-nouns).
* **Day 2:** Sections 10.4 – 10.7 (Separable-prefix verbs, *zu* + infinitive, *um ... zu*, *ohne ... zu*, *statt ... zu*) & Exercises 1 & 2.
* **Day 3:** Sections 10.8 – 10.9 (The passive voice with *werden* and the present perfect with modal verbs).
* **Day 4:** Section 10.10 (The simple past tense of *haben*, *sein*, and modal verbs).
* **Day 5:** Vocabulary & Conversation (*Enquiring about a New Year holiday*).
* **Day 6 & 7:** Weekly revision and audio practice.

### Week 11: Quantities, Adjectival Nouns, Subordinate Clauses, and Reflexive Verbs (Pages 155–178)
* **Goal:** Link complex clauses (moving the verb to the end) and use reflexive pronouns and verbs.
* **Day 1:** Sections 11.1 – 11.2 (Terms expressing quantity and using adjectives as nouns).
* **Day 2:** Sections 11.3 – 11.4 part 1 (Coordinating conjunctions *und/aber/sondern/denn* and relative pronouns *der/die/das/dessen/deren*).
* **Day 3:** Section 11.4 parts 2 & 3 (Subordinating conjunctions *dass, ob, weil, obwohl, während, als, wenn*) & Exercises 1 & 2.
* **Day 4:** Sections 11.5 – 11.6 (Reflexive pronouns *mich/mir/sich* and reflexive verbs).
* **Day 5:** Vocabulary & Conversation (*A customer returns a faulty CD to the shop*).
* **Day 6 & 7:** Weekly revision and subordinate clause word-order drills.

### Week 12: Simple Past, Past Perfect, Subjunctive, and Conditionals (Pages 179–198)
* **Goal:** Master narrative past tenses, conditional statements ('If... then...'), and mood particles.
* **Day 1:** Simple past tense patterns & stem-changing verbs in the past.
* **Day 2:** The past perfect (e.g., 'I had been').
* **Day 3:** The general subjunctive & conditional statements ('If... then...').
* **Day 4:** The passive with *zu* + infinitive & idiomatic modal particles.
* **Day 5:** Week 12 Exercises, vocabulary, and conversation.
* **Day 6 & 7:** Weekly revision and audio repetition.

### Week 13: Participles, Reported Speech, and Course Completion (Pages 199–224)
* **Goal:** Handle advanced constructions like reported speech and participle clauses, and complete the final European travel reading practice.
* **Day 1:** The present participle (-ing form) and alternatives, plus more linking words.
* **Day 2:** Addressing a group informally (*ihr*), subordinate clauses with two infinitives, and impersonal expressions.
* **Day 3:** The special subjunctive, reported speech, and word order in reported speech.
* **Day 4:** Participle clauses & Week 13 Exercises, vocabulary, and conversation.
* **Day 5:** Reading Practice (Pages 223–224): *Eine Reise durch Europa*.
* **Day 6 & 7:** Final course review! Re-listen to all conversations from Weeks 2–13 to test your listening comprehension and speaking fluency.
